Three transactions close the loop

Every settled invoice leaves the same three entries on chain. The pay page links all three, so your client and your accountant can check them without asking you.

1 of 3

The client pays

A plain USDC transfer to the invoice's pay address, from any wallet or exchange. The address is the same on every supported chain and holds no code until it is swept.

Base or Arbitrum
2 of 3

The contract matches it

DaybookReactive is subscribed to transfers into that address. It counts the payment, handles part payments and overpayments, and when the invoice is covered it asks the chain where the money is to settle.

Reactive Network
3 of 3

The till settles

DaybookTill sweeps the address, moves the tax share to a savings vault, pays each team share, sends the rest to the treasury and attests a receipt to the payer through EAS.

Same chain as the payment

Rules you set once

A split policy is fixed when you create it, so an invoice always settles the way it said it would. Tax plus team shares can be at most 100%, with up to five people on the team.
