Three slips for every invoice

Each settled invoice leaves three transactions you can look up. The pay page lists all three, so nobody has to take our word for it.

PaymentBase or Arbitrum

The client pays

A plain USDC transfer to the pay address. Same address on every supported chain, no code there until it is swept.

MatchReactive Network

The contract matches it

DaybookReactive counts the transfer, keeps track of part and over payments, and asks the paying chain to settle once the invoice is covered.

SettlementSame chain

The till pays out

DaybookTill sweeps the address, sends tax to the savings vault, pays the team, sends the rest to the treasury and attests the receipt.

Set the split once

A split policy is fixed at creation. Up to five people on the team, and tax plus team shares can't pass 100%. The rest goes to the treasury.
