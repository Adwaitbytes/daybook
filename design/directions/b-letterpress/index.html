Invoices that mark themselves paid

Send a client one address. They pay in USDC on Base or Arbitrum, from any wallet. A contract matches the payment, sweeps it, puts the tax aside, pays the team and sends a receipt. Nothing of ours has to be running.

One policy per invoice

A split policy cannot change after it is created. Up to five team members; tax and team shares together are at most 100%. Whatever is left goes to the treasury.
