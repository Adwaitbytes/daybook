# Decisions

One entry each, newest last. Format: date, decision, reason.

- 2026-11-19: Pay addresses come from a separate `PayAddressFactory`, not from the till. Reason: `AbstractCallback` stores the callback proxy as an immutable, so the till's init code (and address) must differ per chain. The factory's only constructor argument is the owner, so deployed through the CREATE2 deployer `0x4e59b44847b379578588920cA78FbF26c0B4956C` with a fixed salt it lands at the same address on every payment chain (deployer presence confirmed in ref/FACTS.md section 5; absent on Reactive, which never needs it).
- 2026-11-19: Pay address = CREATE2(factory, keccak256(abi.encode(reactive, invoiceId)), EIP-1167 clone of the vault implementation). The implementation is the factory's first CREATE, so the reactive contract derives it from the factory address alone and needs no extra constructor argument. Salting with the reactive contract's address stops a redeployed reactive contract from reusing old pay addresses.
- 2026-11-19: The vault can only move funds when called by the factory, and the factory only sends to the calling till (`isTill`). Several tills may be authorized at once so a till migration never strands funds. Trade-off: the factory owner can authorize a till, so the owner key is trusted on every payment chain.
- 2026-11-19: Watching mode is per invoice. Default (`strictTokens = false`): one subscription per accepted chain with the contract as wildcard and topic2 = pay address, so wrong-token payments are seen and flagged, and two accepted tokens on one chain cost one subscription. `strictTokens = true` subscribes per (chain, token) exactly as the spec describes. Cost of the default: any token sent to the pay address triggers a reaction we pay for (about 0.04 REACT on mainnet per `estimate_cost`); bounded by recording at most 16 flagged entries per invoice and ignoring zero-value transfers (the address-poisoning pattern) before any storage write.
- 2026-11-19: Overpayment, late payment (after the invoice is covered) and payments after cancellation or expiry are forwarded to the treasury unsplit, and the ledger records them. Refunding to `from` was rejected: the sender is often an exchange hot wallet that would not credit the client. Tax and team splits apply only to the invoice amount, because excess is a liability to the client, not income.
- 2026-11-19: Cancellation and expiry forward any partial payment to the treasury unsplit, so a pay address never keeps money after its invoice closes. Refunds are the owner's decision.
- 2026-11-19: Invoice amounts are stored in 6-decimal units; each accepted pair carries its token's decimals and transfers are normalized before comparison. Settlements always move raw token units on their own chain, so normalization dust never strands funds. Accepted-token transfers above uint96 raw units are flagged instead of counted (no real stablecoin payment is that large), which lets a settlement fit two storage slots.
- 2026-11-19: Each chain's balance is settled on its own chain, one settlement per (invoice, chain, token) with a balance. Partial payments do not trigger callbacks; the reactive contract waits until the invoice is covered.
- 2026-11-19: A settlement request is only started with at least 320,000 gas left in the reaction; otherwise the invoice joins a closing queue drained on the next cron tick. Reason: measured cost per request is about 110,000 to 140,000 gas and the reactive cap is 900,000. An eight-pair cover peaks at about 764,000 in the harness. `MAX_ACCEPTED` is 8.
- 2026-11-19: Settlement storage holds only invoice id, pair index, timing, status and amounts (two slots); chain and token come from the accepted pair and the receipt payer is the pair's latest payer at send time. Reason: the first version stored them and a three-chain cover ran out of the 900,000 cap.
- 2026-11-19: Resend: a settlement without an ack after `resendAfter` (default 300 s, minimum 60 s) is resent on the next cron tick with the same sequence number; after five sends it is marked stuck and leaves the queue (so it no longer holds cron). The owner can `retrySettlement` or `resolveSettlement`. A `ManualSweep` from the till resolves pending settlements for that invoice and token automatically.
- 2026-11-19: Cron tier is a constructor argument. Mainnet uses Cron1000 (about 14 minutes at the measured 0.815 s blocks), Lasna Cron100. Reason: `estimate_cost` on mainnet gives about 14.9 REACT/day for Cron100 and about 1.5 REACT/day for Cron1000 while any timed invoice is open; overdue marking and resends do not need minute precision. Invoices without due or expiry times never hold cron.
- 2026-11-19: Till failures that would block an invoice forever are redirected rather than reverted: a failing team transfer or a reverting reserve sends that share to the treasury with `PayoutRedirected`. A failing receipt issuer is caught (`ReceiptFailed`) and its gas is capped at 250,000. A reserve that succeeds but pulls the wrong amount reverts, since that is a broken integration.
- 2026-11-19: Till guards: per-token settlement cap (zero means the token is disabled, so an unconfigured token cannot be swept by a callback), pause (settle only; owner sweep still works), owner-only rescue and sweep, Ownable2Step, no upgradeability.
- 2026-11-19: Savings vault is a plain holding vault behind `IReserve`. A yield integration on Base waits until it can be verified thoroughly.
- 2026-11-19: Receipts: `EasReceiptIssuer` where EAS is deployed (OP-stack predeploy `0x4200...0021` on Base, Base Sepolia, Unichain Sepolia; `0xbD75...c458` on Arbitrum; per ref/FACTS.md section 4), configured per till; the till's `Receipt` event is the receipt everywhere else.
- 2026-11-19: Default callback gas limit 1,000,000: worst-case `settle` measured at about 426,000 gas, plus the largest measured proxy overhead (Ethereum, 150,000 extraGas plus 50,000 maxChargeGas) plus the 250,000 receipt allowance.
- 2026-11-19: `check_rc_source` flagged three owner functions on the till whose first parameter was an `address` (it treats them as unguarded callbacks). They were false positives, but the parameters were retyped (`IERC20 token`) or reordered (`withdrawNative(amount, to)`) so the check reads clean rather than carrying a justification.
- 2026-11-19: Lints silenced in foundry.toml with reasons (reentrancy-events and reentrancy-no-eth on calls to the system contract and owner-configured contracts, bounded casts, bounded validation loops, block-timestamp by design). `forge build` is warning-free.
- 2026-11-19: The harness models chains inside one EVM. One factory serves all simulated chains (one pay address can only be one account in one EVM); chains stay separate because each has its own token contracts, till and proxy. The mock system contract reports bookkeeping as events instead of storage so the 900,000 gas cap applied to `react()` stays meaningful.
- 2026-11-19: The TypeScript CLI runs on Node's built-in type stripping (no tsx, no build scripts to approve) and never handles a private key: `create-invoice --send` shells out to `cast send --account omni-deployer` with `execFile`.
- 2026-11-19: Local broadcast artifacts are not tracked. Deployment records will go to `evidence/` from real transactions only.
- 2026-11-19: Security review fixes (docs/SECURITY_REVIEW.md). Invoice field `strictTokens` replaced by `watchAllTokens` with the opposite meaning, so the zero value and every default watch accepted tokens only (H-1). Same ABI type, so old encoders silently get the opposite behaviour: the CLI and `CreateInvoice` script were updated in the same change.
- 2026-11-19: A watch-all invoice narrows itself to accepted tokens when its sixteenth wrong-token entry is recorded (H-1). Invoice subscriptions are now derived from the accepted pairs, pay address and watch mode rather than stored, which cut an eight-pair narrowing from over 900k gas to about 510k and keeps subscribe and unsubscribe exact.
- 2026-11-19: Receipt payer is the sender of the largest single counted transfer on the pair, frozen once the invoice closes; late and orphaned settlements pin their own sender (M-2). Chosen over one receipt per payment entry, which would multiply EAS cost.
- 2026-11-19: `RECEIPT_GAS` 400,000 (measured real EAS attest 313,995 on a Base mainnet fork) and default callback gas limit 1,300,000 (M-3). Issuer called with a low-level call capped at a 32-byte return (L-2, and a return-bomb guard).
- 2026-11-19: `setTill` refused while that chain has open settlements, stuck ones included (L-1). The per-chain counter is stored plus one from the first `setTill` so the reaction path never pays a zero-to-nonzero write.
- 2026-11-19: Owner `recordMissedTransfer` shares the dedupe key with `react()` (M-4). `maxInvoiceAmount` mirrors the till caps, default 10,000 USD (L-6). `renounceOwnership` disabled on all four owned contracts (L-3). Manual sweep resolves only what it moved and nothing on zero (L-4), with a gas-bounded queue walk (L-5).
- 2026-11-19: DaybookReactive is compiled through the IR pipeline (foundry `compilation_restrictions`), every other contract with the legacy pipeline. Reason: after the fixes the legacy build was 24,248 bytes, 328 under the limit; IR gives 22,516.
- 2026-11-19: First live deployment (v0, `d8b866b`) kept only for one loop, then superseded by v1 (`adfc5a8`) after the security review; its remaining 0.9 lREACT was withdrawn. Every v0 script after the first invoice ran from a worktree pinned to the deployed commit, because the fixes were being edited into `contracts/src` at the same time and a script compiled against new source silently encoded `watchAllTokens` into v0's `strictTokens` slot.
- 2026-11-19: v1 factory is the via-IR build at `0xA798…D33c`, created by direct calls to the CREATE2 deployer, with tills deployed by `forge create`. Reason: `compilation_restrictions` puts `DeployReactive.s.sol` in the IR profile, so the reactive contract predicted the IR factory while `DeployTill.s.sol` deployed the legacy one (`0xAa4A…1D04`, recorded as superseded v1-a). Redeploying the reactive contract would have cost about 0.5 lREACT; the factory is about 0.0004 ETH per chain. Open for the contract owner: both scripts must take the factory init code from one place (for example a pinned artifact read with `vm.getCode`) so they can never disagree.
- 2026-11-19: Lasna transactions are sent with `--with-gas-price 100gwei --priority-gas-price 1`: the RPC refuses any transaction whose `maxFee × gasLimit` exceeds 1 ether, which forge's default 2× base fee hits on a contract deploy.
- 2026-11-19: Loop latency is reported only within one chain's clock (Base `blockTimestampMs`, 200 ms; Unichain and Lasna 1 s). Lasna block stamps measured 0.9 to 2.2 s behind wall time and Base 0.2 to 0.7 s ahead, so cross-chain stamp differences are kept raw in `evidence/loops.csv` but not called latency.
- 2026-11-19: The loop tracer finds callbacks by the till's `Settled(invoiceId, seq)` logs on the destination, not by the system contract's `getCallbacks`, which stayed empty for every live loop. It takes the first `Settled` per (invoiceId, seq), since v1 re-emits it on duplicate delivery. Costs are balance-plus-reserves-minus-debt deltas across the block, flagged when another transaction of the same payer shares that block.
- 2026-11-19: Live loops use the default accepted-token watch. One deliberate watch-all invoice (v1 #4) was used for the wrong-token test and closed by payment right after.
- 2026-11-19: Deploy scripts fixed (c426893): one pinned factory init code (contracts/script/artifacts/PayAddressFactory.v1.initcode.hex, via-IR build of adfc5a8) used by both DeployReactive and DeployTill; DeployTill calls the Arachnid CREATE2 deployer directly; EXPECTED_FACTORY guard; Lasna gas price capped at 100 gwei in scripts and Makefile. contracts/src unchanged, live v1 stays byte-identical.
- 2026-11-19: Web app in `web/`: Next.js 16.4.6 App Router, React 19.3.1, strict TypeScript (noUncheckedIndexedAccess), Tailwind 4.3.5 with the Ledger tokens as CSS variables mapped through `@theme`, viem 2.58.1, wagmi 3.7.11 (only on /make), TanStack Query 5.108, Motion 14.14 (the stamp only), Biome 2.6.8, Vitest 5.1.1. Fonts are the direction's Libre Caslon Text and DM Mono files through `next/font/local`.
- 2026-11-19: The site takes only the DaybookReactive address from a deployment record (`pnpm gen` reads contracts/deployments/*.json, else docs/DEPLOYMENTS.md, into src/generated/deployments.ts). Factory, tills, policies, invoices, ledger and settlements are read from chain on request, so a stale record cannot show a wrong pay address. No record means a designed "not deployed yet" state. `DAYBOOK_REACTIVE_ADDRESS` previews a real deployment before its record lands; the generated record wins.
- 2026-11-19: The pay page recomputes the pay address from the deployed factory (CREATE2 of the EIP-1167 clone, as scripts/src/payAddress.ts) and refuses to show an address or QR if it differs from the one the contract stores. The QR is an EIP-681 USDC transfer for the selected chain and the balance still due.
- 2026-11-19: Status wording follows the security review: contract status `Paid` shows "Payment seen"; the Paid stamp, receipt and three transaction links appear only on `Settled`.
- 2026-11-19: Chain reads cache what cannot change (ledger lines, which are append-only, event hops, block times, final settlement states, accepted pairs) and serve the rest stale-while-revalidate, because the public Lasna RPC takes one to four seconds a call. Settled, cancelled and expired pay payloads are held 60 s. The callback transaction is found by scanning the till's `Settled` log only in a window around the settlement request (Base Sepolia 200 ms blocks make a full scan impractical).
- 2026-11-19: Health "REACT spent" is measured, not estimated: the drop in the contract's balance plus reserves minus debt across the block of each reaction that emitted an event (the tracer's method). Reactions sharing a block are left out of the sample; cron ticks with no event are not counted, and the page says so. "Reactions left" is spendable funds over the mean measured fee.
- 2026-11-19: Neon Postgres (schema `daybook`, Drizzle, migrations in web/drizzle) holds off-chain invoice details, durable demo rate-limit counters and found callback hops. Every query runs through `withDb` with a 3 s deadline and returns a value instead of throwing, so pay pages, status, ledger and health render from chain alone when the database is down (tests: `db-down.test.ts`; live check: `pnpm test:db`). Details are shown only when their keccak256 equals the invoice's on-chain `detailsHash`. The Neon driver's default `api.` endpoint rewrite does not resolve for this project's host, so the client posts to the endpoint host's own /sql.
- 2026-11-19: "Pay a demo invoice" is `POST /api/demo-pay`, same-origin only. It signs with `DEMO_RELAYER_PK` (a dedicated low-balance wallet, env only). If the relayer owns the demo contract it creates a 0.10 USDC invoice; otherwise it pays the first open id in `DEMO_INVOICE_IDS`. Limits: 2 per IP per 10 min, 6 per IP per day, 30 per hour and 200 per day site-wide, kept in memory and in Postgres; with the database down only the per-instance memory limits apply. With the key unset the route answers 503 `not-configured` and the page shows a designed state.
- 2026-11-19: "Make your own" deploys from the visitor's wallet in eight transactions: DaybookReactive on Lasna (funded 0.5 lREACT, factory address predicted from the owner), the factory through the CREATE2 deployer and a till on Base Sepolia, factory.setTill, till.setMaxSettlement (a till refuses a token with no cap), reactive.setTill, createPolicy and a first invoice. A Lasna hash that the network does not know two minutes later is treated as the MetaMask Smart Transactions failure and the page says to turn the setting off (spec 7.19).
- 2026-11-19: A fixture state gallery at /design/states renders states the live demo may not be in (part paid, wrong token, expired, overdue, wrong network, no wallet, Smart Transactions, rate limited) for screenshots. It is off unless `DAYBOOK_STATE_GALLERY=1` and carries a banner saying the data is fixture data; its pay address is the burn address.
- 2026-11-19: Lighthouse 13.6.0 (headless, default mobile throttling) on the production build reading the live Lasna deployment: home 100 performance, 100 accessibility, 100 best practices (LCP 757 ms, CLS 0); pay page /pay/2 (settled) 100 / 100 / 100 (LCP 755 ms, CLS 0). A cold pay page waits on the public Lasna RPC (one to four seconds a call), which is why final invoices are served from memory.

Reason: on a push event the action scans only the pushed commits, and the standard is no secret anywhere in history.

Reason: the rule is "enforced by a script in CI" (section 15), and identifiers such as `unlock()` in code are not prose. A line can opt out with `writing-check-ignore` plus a reason.

- 2026-11-19 (verify): `make verify` lives in `tools/verify` (TypeScript on Node's type stripping, viem 2.58.1, its own lockfile; the JSON-RPC client, CSV parser and report are copied from Glidepath's verify so each repo stands alone, with the TypeScript parameter properties rewritten because type stripping rejects them). It re-derives every chain field of every `evidence/loops.csv` row from receipts and logs, rebuilds both deployments' ledgers, checks every contract for code and Sourcify status, every transaction hash linked or tabled in README.md, JUDGE.md and docs/*.md, the FIRST_LOOPS latency and cost tables, the deploy gas and deploy cost in DEPLOYMENTS.md, and the live Health page against chain at head. It writes `evidence/verify-report.json` and exits 1 on any mismatch. - 2026-11-19 (verify): Chain-derived numbers in the docs are pinned to a Lasna block recorded in `evidence/summary.json` (`asOf`), with each payment chain pinned at its last block a minute earlier so every transfer inside the scan has been delivered by the Lasna pin. Reason: the live demo keeps settling invoices, so numbers read at head would go stale between `make summary` and `make verify`. Every chain number at the pin is rebuilt from event logs (invoice lifecycle, ledger, settlement requests and acks), so it does not depend on archive state, except the lREACT fee of each reaction, which needs `balance + reserves - debt` at the reaction's block and the block before. If the public Lasna RPC prunes those blocks, verify fails loudly with an RPC error rather than skipping them.
- 2026-11-19 (verify): "Every inflow matched or flagged" is checked by scanning every `Transfer` into and out of every pay address on both payment chains (topic filters on the pay addresses, 200-block chunks on Base Sepolia, 1,000 on Unichain Sepolia) and classifying each inflow: matched to a ledger entry, flagged `WrongToken`, a non-accepted token on an accepted-token invoice (not watched by design, so never delivered), sent after the invoice closed, zero-value, or unrecorded. Only "unrecorded" is a mismatch. Settled pay addresses must net to zero in every accepted token; non-accepted tokens left there (the two DBKTEST test transfers) are listed. Each settlement's split is recomputed from its policy (floors per share, treasury takes the remainder plus excess) and compared per recipient with the transfers in its callback. Cost of the approach: a fresh scan grows with time (Base Sepolia makes 432,000 blocks a day, so about 2,200 calls per scan per day of history); the cache keeps reruns fast.
- 2026-11-19 (verify): The Health numbers verify publishes are the page's own method (web/src/lib/server/views.ts): running since the first invoice's `createdAt` (the deployment record has no deploy block), invoice counts from status, lREACT spent as the sum of measured fees of reactions that wrote a ledger line or an ack, at most the newest 60, leaving out any that shares a block with another transaction of the contract. The live page is compared at head with one re-read, since a stranger may pay a demo invoice between the two reads.
